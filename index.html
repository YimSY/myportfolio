From Engineer to Educator to Designer

My journey isn't linear, and that's my superpower. I started in Mechanical Engineering, learning the rigour of how things work. I then moved into Education, mastering how people think and learn.

Now, as a Product Designer, I bridge these worlds. I don't just design screens; I design systems that are technically feasible (thanks to engineering) and deeply empathetic to the user's learning curve (thanks to teaching).
